Allocation Configuration

Priority First strategy sorts all orders by their auto-computed Priority Score. Available stock is distributed sequentially, satisfying higher priority orders in full first before remaining stock is given to lower priority orders.

Stock Shortage Rules

If total order requirements exceed available warehouse inventory:

1. System flags shortage exception automatically.
2. Allocates maximum partial amounts to priority lines.
3. Posts replenishment decisions to Decision Center.
4. Holds affected order dispatches pending stock resolution.
