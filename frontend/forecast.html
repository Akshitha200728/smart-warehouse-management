Forecasting Logic Explanation

Runout Time Formula:
$$\text{Days to Stockout} = \frac{\text{Available Stock}}{\text{Average Daily Demand}}$$
Risk Classification Matrix:
  • Critical Risk: Runout Time < Supplier Lead Time. (A stockout is guaranteed because new inventory cannot be delivered before stock reaches 0).
  • High Risk: Runout Time ≤ Lead Time + 3 days.
  • Medium Risk: Runout Time ≤ Lead Time + 7 days.
  • Low Risk: Stock levels are sufficient.
